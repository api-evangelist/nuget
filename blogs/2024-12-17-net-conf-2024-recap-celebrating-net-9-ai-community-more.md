<p>At <a href="https://www.dotnetconf.net/">.NET Conf 2024</a> we celebrated the official launch of .NET 9 alongside groundbreaking announcements across the entire .NET ecosystem and a deeper dive into the world of .NET for developers worldwide. Organized by Microsoft and the .NET community, the event was a huge success, providing .NET developers with 3 days of incredible, free .NET content. For the first time ever, this year also included a bonus &#8220;day 4&#8221; of YouTube premieres following the initial 3 days, which brought even more great content to .NET developer.</p>

<h2>On-Demand Recordings</h2>
<p>If you missed the event, feel free to catch up on the sessions via our on-demand playlists on <a href="https://aka.ms/dotnetconf24/OnDemand">YouTube</a> or <a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/shows/dotnet-conf-2024/">Microsoft Learn</a>.</p>
<p></p>
<p>This year, we streamed 92 sessions over 4 days with most of those sessions delivered live.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Day 1</strong> featured the official release of .NET 9, including a 1-hour keynote and sessions led by the .NET team to introduce new features and enhancements related to .NET 9 including topics like .NET Aspire, AI, .NET MAUI, web development, Visual Studio, and more.</li>
<li><strong>Day 2</strong> provided a deeper dive into .NET capabilities, continuously broadcast for 24 hours to reach all time zones.</li>
<li><strong>Day 3</strong> was a continuation of the 24-hour broadcast, offering a wide range of sessions from speakers around world.</li>
<li><strong>Day 4</strong> was a new &#8220;bonus&#8221; addition this year, which included pre-recorded YouTube premieres that included a range of topics from the .NET community.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Upskill on .NET Aspire</h2>
<p>.NET Aspire training and credential on Microsoft Learn: To earn the <a href="https://aka.ms/aspire-credential">Build distributed apps with .NET Aspire credential</a> learners demonstrate the ability to build distributed apps with .NET Aspire. Through the training and credential, learners will learn the following:</p>
<ul>
<li>Add .NET Aspire to a solution</li>
<li>Configure service discovery</li>
<li>Configure components</li>
<li>Monitor resources with the .NET Aspire dashboard</li>
<li>Create tests with .NET Aspire</li>
<li>Prepare for deployment</li>
</ul>
<p>.NET Aspire for Beginners video series: Are you completely new to .NET Aspire? This <a href="https://aka.ms/dotnet/beginnervideos/aspire">beginner video series</a> teaches you how to get started with .NET Aspire and implement it into your applications.</p>
<h2>Customer Stories</h2>
<p>There was an astonishing amount of customer evidence presented this year at .NET Conf including some exciting videos and mentions during the keynote presentation. Please see below for some of the customer evidence highlights.</p>
<p><strong>Microsoft Copilot</strong></p>
<p>Discover how a small team of five developers at Microsoft transformed the Copilot backend in just four months using .NET &amp; .NET Aspire. Join Pedram Rezaei, a developer on the Copilot backend team, as he shares their journey to improve performance, scalability, and reliability for millions of users worldwide.</p>
<p>Whether you&#8217;re a .NET developer or interested in building scalable, reliable services efficiently, this inspiring story demonstrates what&#8217;s possible with the right tools and a dedicated team.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Fidelity Investments</strong></p>
<p>Discover Fidelity&#8217;s latest innovation in trading technology with Active Trader Pro, built on Microsoft&#8217;s .NET MAUI platform. This powerful, cross-platform trading solution brings seamless performance to both Windows and Mac users, backed by real-time data streaming, advanced tools, and Microsoft’s support. Join Fidelity’s SVP Mark Burns as he shares how .NET MAUI enables Fidelity to deliver a fast, reliable, and scalable experience for active traders everywhere.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Chevron Phillips Chemical</strong></p>
<p>We partnered with Chevron Phillips Chemical Company to showcase their migration story with .NET and Azure. We presented the slide below during the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ikSNL-lxolc&amp;list=PLdo4fOcmZ0oXeSG8BgCVru3zQtw_K4ANY&amp;index=5">keynote at .NET Conf 2024</a> and also had their Cloud Architect Manager present live at our <a href="https://ignite.microsoft.com/sessions/BRK149?source=sessions">.NET session at Ignite</a>.</p>
<p><strong>KPMG</strong></p>
<p>KPMG is another company we have been partnering with to promote the positive outcomes of utilizing .NET and Azure, specifically for KPMG Clara. We showcased the below slide in the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ikSNL-lxolc&amp;list=PLdo4fOcmZ0oXeSG8BgCVru3zQtw_K4ANY&amp;index=5">.NET Conf keynote</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Xbox</strong></p>
<p>The Xbox team recently started using .NET Aspire in Xbox services as they are going through a large migration to the latest .NET. They shared how .NET Aspire has helped them speed up and tighten their inner development loop.</p>
